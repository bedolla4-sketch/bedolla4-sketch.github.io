Nutritional Foraging Strategies for Exotics

In the wild, exotic animals spend most of their awake hours hunting or searching for meals. These setups help replicate those natural habits.

Puzzle Logs & Crevice Hiding

Placing insects, seeds, or small treats deeps inside safe cork bark pockets or drilled timber tubes. This prompts small mammals and reptiles to scratch, chew, or use thier tongues to forage.

Suspended Skewers & Foraging Balls

Hanging fresh leafy greens, fruits, or safe branches high up in bird or small mammal enclosures. This requires them to stretch, balance, and work for their food instead of eating from a floor dish.
