Nutritional Foraging Strategies for Exotics

In the wild, exotic animals spend most of their awake hours hunting or searching for meals. These setups help replicate those natural habits.

Puzzle Logs & Crevice Hiding

Place insects, seeds, or suitable treats inside safe cork bark pockets or purpose-built foraging tubes. These activities encourage animals to explore and search for food.

Suspended Skewers & Foraging Balls

Offer suitable leafy greens or other approved foods in safe, securely positioned enrichment feeders. These activities can encourage exploration, movement, and natural feeding behaviors.
